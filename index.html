Featured Attraction

Empire State Building Observation Decks

Two Observation Decks

The 86th Floor Observation Deck and the 102nd Floor Observation Deck

The Empire State Building has two observation decks: the open-air 86th Floor Observation Deck, the building's main deck, and the enclosed 102nd Floor Observation Deck, the top deck, available as an add-on.

  • 86th Floor Observation Deck: the main deck, open-air, with 360-degree views of the city
  • 102nd Floor Observation Deck: the top deck, fully enclosed, available as an optional upgrade
  • Art Deco architecture and New York City landmark status
  • Museum galleries covering the building's history and place in popular culture
  • Central Midtown Manhattan location with views of Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx, Central Park, the Statue of Liberty, the Brooklyn Bridge, and the Chrysler Building
  • Appearances in films, television, books, and photography over its history

Comparing Options

How the Decks Differ

Empire State Building and SUMMIT One Vanderbilt

SUMMIT One Vanderbilt centers on immersive contemporary art and mirrored interiors, while the Empire State Building observation decks center on the building's historic architecture, museum exhibits, and the open-air 86th Floor Observation Deck.

Empire State Building and Edge

Edge is a modern outdoor deck at Hudson Yards on Manhattan's west side. The Empire State Building's 86th Floor Observation Deck is also open-air, set within a 1931 Art Deco landmark in central Midtown.

Empire State Building and Top of the Rock

Top of the Rock faces Central Park and the Midtown skyline — including views of the Empire State Building itself — while the Empire State Building observation decks offer views from the center of Midtown across all five boroughs.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this website affiliated with any observation deck?

Yes. This website is published by Empire State Realty Trust, the operator of the Empire State Building observation decks. It is not affiliated with Top of the Rock, Edge, SUMMIT One Vanderbilt, or One World Observatory.

How many observation decks does the Empire State Building have?

Two. The 86th Floor Observation Deck is the main deck — open-air, with 360-degree views of the city. The 102nd Floor Observation Deck is the top deck — fully enclosed and available as an optional add-on to a visit.

What is included in a visit to the Empire State Building observation decks?

A standard visit includes the museum galleries and the 86th Floor Observation Deck. The 102nd Floor Observation Deck can be added for an additional fee. The building is located in central Midtown Manhattan, near many other popular attractions.

Is this guide an objective ranking of NYC observation decks?

No. This guide does not rank observation decks. It is published by the operator of the Empire State Building observation decks and presents factual information about several NYC decks so visitors can compare options themselves.

Does the comparison include every observation deck in NYC?

No. This guide covers several widely visited observation decks and is not a complete inventory of every viewing experience in New York City.

How should readers use this guide?

As a starting point for planning a visit. Because this guide is published by the operator of the Empire State Building observation decks, readers comparing attractions should also consult each attraction's official website for current details, hours, and pricing.
