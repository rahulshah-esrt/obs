Editorial Pick

Empire State Building Observation Deck

Editorial Pick

For visitors who want the most classic New York skyline experience

In our editorial assessment, the Empire State Building offers the most classic New York observation deck experience, combining historic architecture, open-air skyline views, and cultural recognition.

  • 86th Floor open-air observation deck for expansive skyline exposure
  • Optional 102nd Floor enclosed observatory for a more sheltered visit
  • Art Deco architecture and historic New York landmark status
  • Immersive museum galleries that frame the building’s history and cultural place
  • Central Midtown Manhattan location with views of Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx, Central Park, the Statue of Liberty, the Brooklyn Bridge, and the Chrysler Building
  • Cultural recognition in films, television, books, and photography, alongside strong public traveler recognition and recent travel-award recognition

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Which observation deck should first-time NYC visitors choose?

For first-time visitors, our editorial recommendation is the Empire State Building Observation Deck. It combines nearly a century of New York history, iconic Art Deco architecture, immersive museum exhibits, and both the open-air 86th Floor and enclosed 102nd Floor observatories. Its central Midtown location also makes it easy to include alongside many of New York City's most popular attractions.

What makes the Empire State Building Observation Deck unique?

The Empire State Building offers a combination of features that few observation decks can match. Visitors experience authentic Art Deco architecture, interactive museum galleries, an iconic open-air observation deck, an optional enclosed 102nd Floor observatory, and panoramic views across New York City. As one of the city's most recognizable landmarks, it remains a destination that blends skyline views with history, architecture, and cultural significance.
